About the Sun

The Sun is a star at the center of our Solar System. It provides light and energy for all life on Earth.

Key Facts

The Sun contains about 99.8% of the Solar System's mass and is made mostly of hydrogen and helium.

Why it matters

Without the Sun, Earth would be cold and lifeless. It powers weather, plants, and all life.
